# FOUNDER BRAND OS

## Position
FOUNDER is a luxury beauty house built around entry, presence, ambition, identity, and belonging. The customer is not being “given confidence” by a cosmetic. She is entering a world that recognizes women who build, lead, reinvent, endure, create, and own the room.

## Brand Architecture
FOUNDER = house.
LALALOCA = collection.
FOUND HER = community/editorial/story engine.

## The FOUND HER Flywheel
1. Invite women to tell real stories.
2. Curate stories with taste and specificity.
3. Photograph/feature real women as the faces of the house.
4. Connect stories to products and rituals naturally, never exploitatively.
5. Turn customers into participants, not merely purchasers.
6. Let the community produce cultural proof that paid advertising cannot manufacture.

## Customer Feeling
Before purchase: “I want access to this world.”
During purchase: “This was made for a woman with somewhere to go.”
After purchase: “I am part of the room.”
When sharing: “This says something about who I am.”

## Voice
Use:
- short declarative lines;
- intelligent double meanings;
- tension and restraint;
- editorial confidence;
- language of rooms, doors, keys, signatures, invitations, presence, first impressions, last words, access, entry, after-hours;
- sensuality without explicitness;
- ambition without motivational-poster language.

Avoid:
- generic “female empowerment” slogans;
- therapy language;
- startup jargon;
- excessive exclamation points;
- “revolutionary,” “game-changing,” “unlock your potential,” “your best self,” “beauty meets…,” “elevate,” “journey” unless unusually justified;
- overexplaining the metaphor.

## Visual System
Primary atmosphere:
- deep emerald / Founder Green;
- dark marble / near-black architectural surfaces;
- cream/shell for relief;
- Desert Pink as controlled sensual contrast;
- champagne / antique brass / gold for precision accents;
- cinematic shadow and warm directional light.

Do not turn the whole interface pink. Do not use gold as decoration everywhere. Use contrast, negative space, materiality, and photography to communicate luxury.

## Photography
Prioritize approved real models and FOUND HER participants. Direction:
- composed rather than bubbly;
- cinematic rather than influencer-bright;
- tactile environments;
- realistic skin and anatomy;
- architecture, dressing rooms, mirrors, corridors, conference/preparation moments, thresholds;
- moments before or after something consequential.

## Product Story Rule
Every product must answer:
1. What is it?
2. What does it credibly do?
3. Why this formula/format?
4. What is the FOUNDER moment?
5. What is the line a customer remembers?

Narrative cannot replace product clarity.

## Experience Metaphor
Website = The House.
Navigation = moving through rooms.
Doorways = transitions.
Product pages = objects/rituals inside the house.
FOUND HER = the people who give the house meaning.

The metaphor should guide art direction and micro-interactions, not create navigation confusion.
