# FOUNDER Launch-Day Runbook — October 1, 2026

## Purpose

Keep launch day operationally simple. No major redesigns, naming changes, pricing experiments, new bundles, or discounting after this point unless a customer-blocking issue requires it.

## Before traffic

- Confirm the latest Vercel production deployment is green.
- Open the homepage, FOUNDER Collection, LALALOCA shop, all eight PDPs, FOUND HER, shipping and returns on desktop and a phone-sized viewport.
- Test one full purchase path from each collection:
  - FOUNDER Collection product → bag → Shopify checkout.
  - LALALOCA serum → bag → Shopify checkout.
  - House Trio → bag → Shopify checkout.
  - Smooth Talker: test all three shades.
- Confirm sold-out behavior blocks purchase when Shopify reports a variant unavailable.
- Confirm prices displayed on-site match Shopify.
- Confirm free US shipping and one-business-day dispatch language are consistent.
- Confirm email signup reaches Shopify and the welcome email is sent.
- Confirm no customer-facing preorder or reservation copy remains.

## Launch sequence

1. Threshold teaser: **The door opens today.**
2. Main reveal: **The FOUNDER Collection has arrived.**
3. Collection overview: five pieces, one routine — cleanse, wash, moisturise, eyes, finish.
4. Founder message: why FOUNDER exists and what “Beauty for what you’re building” means.
5. Product stories: one consequential moment per product, not five vanity shots.
6. FOUND HER: position the brand as a house with culture, not only a product catalog.
7. Evening reminder: one direct Shop the FOUNDER Collection CTA.

## Visual rule for launch content

Vanity is one room, not the entire house. Prioritize:
- backstage preparation
- hotel departure
- boardroom / worktable
- dressing room
- studio
- stage
- travel
- bathroom
- infrastructure / barns
- consequential before-and-after moments

Use approved packaging exactly. No invented label copy, sizes, formulas, or claims.

## Claims and product truth

- Cosmetic language only.
- No unsupported duration, clinical, medical, SPF, physiological, or guaranteed-result claims.
- Smooth Talker is not a sunscreen.
- Shopify is transactional source of truth for current price and inventory.
- If a product reaches zero inventory and Shopify marks it unavailable, the website should show Sold out rather than continue selling.

## Monitoring during launch

Watch:
- product views
- add to cart
- bag views
- begin checkout
- Shopify completed orders
- signup failures
- server errors
- customer questions that indicate copy confusion

If checkout or inventory behavior is broken, fix immediately. If a visual detail is merely imperfect, log it for the post-launch pass rather than destabilizing the site during traffic.

## Do not change on launch day without founder approval

- price
- bundles or discounts
- product names
- formulas or ingredient claims
- legal/policy text
- FOUND HER publication consent state
- StandUp for Kids pledge language
- core brand architecture
