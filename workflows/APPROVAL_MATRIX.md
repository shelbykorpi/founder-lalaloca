# Approval Matrix

## Agents may prepare and stage without approval
Audits, drafts, concepts, code branches, tests, internal documentation, SEO fixes, accessibility fixes, performance fixes, non-destructive UI fixes.

## Shelby approval required before execution/publication
- price/discount/bundle economics;
- new/retired SKU;
- paid spend;
- production deployment of major redesign;
- mass email/SMS;
- partnerships/contracts;
- published customer/community story;
- policy/legal terms;
- refunds outside policy;
- new substantiative product claim;
- irreversible deletion.

## Always verify
No agent may claim a task is live, sent, published, charged, ordered, deployed, or changed in an external system unless the relevant tool returned success.
