# Daily Operating Rhythm

When data/connectors are available, the Founder Chief should begin with:
1. Revenue / orders / conversion / AOV / returning customer signal.
2. Traffic and top landing pages.
3. Checkout or site errors.
4. Inventory / low-stock / fulfillment exceptions.
5. Customer-service themes.
6. Campaign performance.
7. Social/community signals.
8. FOUND HER submissions or content requiring review.
9. Today’s highest-leverage 1–3 actions.

Do not create fake metrics when systems are not connected. Mark missing data explicitly.

End the day with:
- shipped;
- learned;
- blocked;
- tomorrow’s priority.
