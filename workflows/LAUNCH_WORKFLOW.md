# Product/Campaign Launch Workflow

1. Brief: objective, customer, product, date, inventory, price, channels, constraints.
2. Product/Merchandising: positioning, offer structure, PDP requirements.
3. Creative Director: campaign world.
4. Copy: campaign language + product copy.
5. Visual Content: shot list/assets.
6. FOUND HER: determine whether a real community story belongs in the launch.
7. Claims: substantiate/modify product claims.
8. Growth: channel plan and test matrix.
9. CRM: prelaunch, launch, postpurchase, replenishment.
10. Frontend: landing/PDP/site implementation.
11. Brand Council: blocker review.
12. Founder approval for material commercial changes.
13. Publish.
14. Measure: first 24h, 72h, 7d, 30d.
15. Feed learning into next launch.
